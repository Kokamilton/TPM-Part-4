### 1. [ACORN — Founding Product Owner, 0→1 Platform Build](./case-studies/01-acorn-founding-product-owner.md)

> *American Express · 2017–2022*

The foundational case study. Built American Express's cloud-native external collections platform from scratch as the Founding and sole US-based Product Owner — scaling from a 12-entity MVP to a 38-entity production platform supporting $1.3BN in annual US collections across 5.3M accounts and 7 global markets. **What this demonstrates:** 0→1 platform ownership · Data architecture at global scale · Multi-stakeholder alignment under pressure · Enterprise program growth

### 2. [CFPB Ops Metrics — Regulatory Compliance Delivery Under a Fixed Deadline](./case-studies/02-cfpb-ops-metrics.md)

> *American Express · 2020–2021*

Sole Product Owner on the Ops Metrics workstream — a CFPB-mandated data capture capability with a non-negotiable November 30, 2021 regulatory deadline. Designed a 12-entity JSON schema architecture, onboarded 18 outside agencies through parallel bi-directional test-driven development, and delivered on time. The architecture was adopted as the technical baseline for the entire US ACORN platform.

**What this demonstrates:** Regulatory product development · JSON schema design · Parallel multi-partner integration · Compliance delivery under fixed deadline · Strategic decision-making under external pressure

---

### 3. [FICO → Jonas — Vendor Crisis Resolution Mid-Compliance Audit](./case-studies/03-fico-jonas-vendor-crisis.md)

> *American Express · 2021*

Discovered an undisclosed vendor acquisition — FICO had divested its Collections & Recovery business to Jonas Software — mid-compliance audit, through FICO's resistance to providing documentation. Owned full resolution: tri-party NDA coordination, enterprise vendor onboarding, dual contract execution, risk assessment, due diligence, and ORC certification — all while Jonas simultaneously threatened to discontinue support for two production systems.

**What this demonstrates:** Vendor risk management · Compliance crisis ownership · Principal-level decision-making · Multi-track execution under legal and operational pressure
